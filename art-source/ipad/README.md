# iPad background provenance

Six built-in ImageGen edits; exact prompts live in each mission folder. Before
images/prompts/XML are retained here. `generated.png` is the unmodified selected
1448×1086 output. `background.png` and `background@2x.png` are sips resolution
exports at 1368×1026 and 2736×2052. The latter is interpolated, not newly authored
Retina detail. No scripted painting, cropping, icon/state generation or geometry
warping was used to produce the background artwork.

Five scenes translate the original world by (1,129). First Rescue needed a
measured y transform of 1.10*y+165, with x+1, to follow the generated canyon roads;
its traffic links were preserved and the complete playthrough rerun.

The source of First Rescue is the local licensed RT5 `texture/back/103_0.jpg`;
it is not copied here. Its output is a derivative and retains that provenance.

Two corridor samples narrowed during outpainting (Ancient Echoes 20 units,
Twisted Tracks 16 units). These deviations are recorded rather than silently
asserting the old 24±2 measurements still apply. Routing and blocker radii are
unchanged; actual game renders and ordinary completion were checked.
