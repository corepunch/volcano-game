Twisted Tracks: clear the park roads

Current design direction: classic ground-road obstacle clearing.
This supersedes the vertical-rescue and construction-kit concepts as a proposed
gameplay direction. Those earlier drawings remain archived for visual reference.
Concept only: no game code, level routes, production art or animation is changed.

Generation
Built-in ImageGen edit using levels/background-history/twisted-tracks-expanded.png
as the primary environment, with twisted-tracks-vertical-rescue-v2.png as a style
reference only. Exact prompt is saved alongside this concept. Keep the dense
coaster wreck, red/yellow structures, blue train, pond, carousel and lush planting.

Core loop
An existing park road is blocked by a discrete fallen coaster component.
Click the obstruction, send a worker, pay the ordinary resource cost, complete
the timed clearing job and reopen the road. Survivors leave along the cleared
ground route. Different objects use the same familiar interaction.

Four themed obstacles
- Short detached red track panel, visibly snapped from the surrounding coaster.
- Compact broken yellow support section with a cracked concrete collar.
- Bent fence and torn gate, continuing from the park enclosure beside the road.
- Detached blue car and wheel assembly, separate from the static background train.
Small jobs can use the ordinary light-obstruction economy; heavier support/car
jobs can use the existing costly-obstruction pattern. Exact costs are undecided.
Do not add a parts resource, specialist inventory, construction menu or vehicle
dependency just to distinguish these obstacle appearances.

Geography and art rules
- Everyone travels at ground level on established visitor and maintenance roads.
- Keep perimeter paths and add access only where an old lane can plausibly
  continue between the existing foundations and wreckage.
- Show road edges on both sides of each blocker. Clearing it reveals the same
  road underneath rather than creating a new road over the wreck.
- A blocker fills the narrow lane; adjacent wreckage/fencing prevents bypasses.
- A short track piece can be a job; a whole loop or support tower stays scenery.
- Overhead track may remain where real clearance is obvious, but the prototype
  should favor visually open lanes and avoid complex foreground occlusion.
- Survivors stand in ground pockets connected to roads, not on rails or cars.
- Match obstacle paint, lighting and shadows to nearby static debris. Keep torn
  rail ends, bolts and concrete chips beside roads to explain each fallen piece.
- Runtime obstacles must ultimately be separate from the background; once
  cleared, their visible footprint must expose clean underlying road artwork.
- No ladders, catwalks, elevated routes, construction kits or new-road building.

First playable prototype scope
Retain the existing worker queue, clearing and rescue formula. Start with one
short road branch and two coaster-themed blockers, then add visual variants and
further rescue pockets after checking route gating, costs and removal artwork.
Any production prop/state artwork must follow the project's single-atlas rule.
The concept's small obstacle studies are visual references, not extracted assets.

Visual review
The generated image preserves the dense red/yellow coaster, blue train, pond
and carousel, removes elevated rescue structures, and depicts four themed
clearance jobs with matching small studies in the same generated sheet.
Workers and the visible visitor pockets are at ground level. The support job
is placed among rubble from the ride, the fence joins the enclosure, and the
detached car matches the main train. This remains a conceptual layout: final
lane widths, all survivor approaches and obstacle bypasses need measurement
and gameplay checks before using it as a production background.

Validation for future implementation
Check the actual painting, not requested coordinates; keep working positions on
the reachable side, prevent unintended graph bypasses, and verify survivors can
return after each gate opens. Check that clearing a job removes its whole visible
blockage without erasing neighboring background wreckage. Retain sufficient
reachable resources to finish mandatory gates in reasonable spending orders.
