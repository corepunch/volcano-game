Twisted Tracks: vertical rescue concept

Status: visual proposal only. No level, movement, obstacle, animation or renderer
changes are implemented by this concept. The pose study is not a playable
animation or a production sprite atlas.

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, using levels/twisted-tracks.png as
the edit target. Exact prompt is saved alongside the concept image. The original
background remains the source of the landscape and coaster composition.

Playable geography
- Ground: open the bent fence, brace a fallen yellow support, then pass beneath
  the wreck to a survivor pocket. Cyan marks this route in the concept.
- Raised: secure a ladder, climb onto a maintenance landing, repair a short gap
  with boards, then reach people stranded beside the blue train. Amber marks
  this route. The wider concept includes an eastern descent to form a loop.
- Walking surfaces need stable landings and supports. Bare coaster rails and
  vertical loops remain scenery. Boards fill short gaps rather than every route.

Themed clearance jobs
- Bent fence: cut/open the enclosure entrance.
- Tangled cables: isolate and clear a junction.
- Fallen support: jack and brace the beam to open a passage beneath it.
- Jammed blue car: winch it away from the landing.
Costs, tools and job durations are deliberately undecided at concept stage.

Suggested first playable slice
One ground approach, one braced underpass, one ladder with upper/lower landings,
one short repairable bridge and two rescue points (one below, one above).
Climb sequence: approach and align, mount, alternating hands/feet on rungs,
step onto landing, resume walking. Descending must work for the return escort.
Ladder movement should reserve the link so two actors do not overlap on it.

Engine requirements to resolve during prototyping
- Explicit ground/raised route connectivity: screen-space crossings must not
  automatically join routes at different heights.
- Workers and escorted survivors must render behind foreground beams below the
  structure and above decking on the raised route. The current baked background
  alone cannot supply this foreground occlusion; selected overlays/masks or
  separated local wreck pieces will be needed.
- A broken bridge blocks only its own elevated link. Ladder access and return
  travel must remain consistent for both workers and survivors.
- Pose study and obstacle sketches are design references, not extracted assets.
  Author any production animation and prop artwork separately as appropriate.
