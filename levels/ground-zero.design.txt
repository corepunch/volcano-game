GROUND ZERO — reference and mission brief

A fictional Rescue Team mission inspired by aerial views of the World Trade
Center site during September 2001 rescue/recovery operations. Rescue counts,
people, paths and playable logistics are invented; this is not a reconstruction
of individual historical rescues.

Photographic references inspected 2026-10-19:
1. U.S. Navy / Chief Photographer's Mate Eric J. Tilford, September 17, 2001,
   010917-N-7479T-514. Large oblique aerial: dusty steel/concrete debris field,
   damaged surrounding buildings, standing facade fragments, cranes, smoke.
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:010917-N-7479T-514_WTC_Ground_Zero_Aerial.jpg
2. Library of Congress, Witness and Response, digital aerial series. New York
   State Office for Technology / EarthData International, September 15 and 26,
   2001. Overhead geography, surrounding street grid and cleared access lanes.
   https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/911/911-maps.html#obj004
   September 26 image: https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/911/images/lg-map-aerial3.jpg
3. FEMA / Michael Rieger, September 27, 2001, aerial series; FEMA / Andrea
   Booher, October 4 and 8. Additional views of cranes and working lanes.
   https://publicintelligence.net/911-ground-zero-high-resolution-aerial-photos/

Source photos are research references, not the game's background. The new
painted plate and all props are generated with built-in ImageGen. No photographic
people are reused, and no bodies or injuries are depicted. Original UI is reused.

ECONOMY AND OBJECTIVE
Two starting workers, 40 food, no wood/money/fuel. Nine civilians plus one
stranded rescue worker; two animals. IDs deliberately match the existing larger
mission test contract, with a dedicated mode for this mission.
20: salvage timber on western lane (400,530), costs 20 food, yields 20 wood.
21: concrete debris at (345,290), costs 20 food; western rescue pocket beyond.
22: collapsed street debris at (520,620), costs 20 food; 3 x 20 timber caches
    beyond, plus production station access. It leaves the western cafe reachable.
23: heavy steel/concrete at (1070,535), costs 40 food + 100 money.
24: repairable broken paving at (1155,450), costs 20 wood; east rescue pocket.
25: compact concrete debris at (710,410), costs 20 food; central rescue pocket.
Food station repair 20 wood, timber station 20, logistics station 30.
All produce original resources at original intervals. Preserve original costs,
queue semantics, collision and removal, using authored presentation overrides
for urban debris and field stations. Generated prop states together in one atlas.

A plausible depleted opening clears 20 and 22 (food=0), collects 60 timber plus
20 salvaged timber, repairs food station for 20 wood, resumes queued work through
renewable food. Three production repair orders must complete with no grants.
Gates 21/25/23+24 isolate the three rescue pockets. No central perimeter-only
layout: crews use three actual routes within the destroyed complex.
