Twisted Tracks: playable interior roads

Approved concept: concepts/twisted-tracks-interior-roads.png.
Production plate: twisted-tracks-interior.png (two built-in ImageGen edits;
exact prompts alongside). The original twisted-tracks.png remains archived.
Eight custom debris variants were authored together in one transparent ImageGen
atlas, twisted-tracks-debris.png; regions/pivots are in its atlas JSON. No visual
states were synthesized. Cleared objects disappear entirely.

Road layout
The southwest arrival forks toward the central maintenance junction and a lower
supply road. The central junction branches to an upper interior pocket, visitors
inside the broken loop and a train-side pocket. A western branch reaches a cat.
The southern service loop has three producer pads and a second approach toward
the train spur; the expensive car gate is on the shared spur so the loop cannot
bypass it. An extra eastern supply spur is optional.

Jobs and economy
Eighteen local blockers use eight visual families: timber maintenance panels, snapped
yellow supports, enclosure fencing, a blue car, cables, red track, wheels and
reinforced concrete. Ordinary underlying barrier rules retain their established
costs and work animations. The timber panels cost 20 food and yield 20 wood;
other small jobs cost 20 food. The car costs 40 food plus 100 money.
The concrete job is a small broken footing, so it uses a small clearing cost.
Mission archetypes extending tree/stone/boulder, with art from sprites.xml, give
each job its actual debris appearance and title while preserving obstacle
collision, queueing, cost and removal rules.

Two starting workers have 40 food and no wood or money. Clearing the inward
panels and service-road fence can spend all food; the panels' 20 timber plus
three 20-timber caches make food recovery possible. The cafe costs 20 wood to
repair, the sawmill 20 and park office 30. All three supply renewable resources.
Nine civilians plus a stranded technician count toward ten people; the cat and
dog have a separate two-animal goal. Every rescue starts gated.

Checks
The existing coaster playthrough deliberately retains stable objective/producer
IDs and exercises the revised source, road graph, jobs and atlas in the real
runtime. It covers a zero-food opening, queued-job recovery, three repair orders,
all ten people and two animals, nonnegative inventory, pause and restart.
Inspect start and repaired screenshots in build/ after running
VOLCANO_TEST_SHOTS=1 make test-missions (mode --coaster in tests/missions_main.c).
make app APP_MISSION=twisted-tracks builds build/Twisted Tracks.app.
The dedicated tests/coaster_main.c runner also exercises all eight clickable
atlas regions, clears every job before the final rescue, checks full sprite and
click-target removal, and verifies the final car reserves 40 food/100 money once.

Building placement
Each building stands on the centre of its painted pad, measured from
background.png: the dirt pads were segmented by colour, road necks removed, and
each centre taken from the pad's widest contiguous rows (then checked by eye
against the edges). Positions (bottom-left world units): HQ (147,139), cafe
(499,100), sawmill (914,85), park office (1127,101.5). A position is the base
centre: the engine centres every building state and its selection ring there,
so no coordinate compensates for a sprite's pivot. Starting crew and the final
approach node of each spur sit at their buildings; the HQ spur keeps an
intermediate node so its last link stays short. Outputs stay outside the
building footprints.
